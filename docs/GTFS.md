# BTD timetable import

Brazos Transit District publishes the source feed at https://www.btd.org/2025btdgtfs/. The checked snapshot is `data/transit/btd-2026-10-15.zip`, downloaded October 15, 2026. Its feed version is “Updated: Mar 25, 2026, 2:20 PM”, and its declared coverage is July 15, 2025 through December 31, 2026 in America/Chicago. Attribution: Brazos Transit District. No explicit redistribution license was found in the downloaded feed; attribution is not a license grant.

Run `python3 scripts/import-btd.py` to reproduce `src/data/btd-timetable.json` and `data/transit/provenance.json` without network access. An identical copy of the reviewed feed can be supplied with `--input /path/to/feed.zip`. The CLI refuses different bytes until the checked-in snapshot, source dates, holiday overlay, and evidence have been deliberately reviewed and updated. The importer checks ZIP integrity and records the SHA-256 of the exact input bytes. A new snapshot requires editorial review of source dates, holiday overlays and conflicts; the fixed checkedOn date describes this reviewed snapshot, not an automatically verified new download.

The importer validates agency identity and timezone, feed/calendar dates, all stop coordinates, unique IDs, trip/route/service/stop references, nonnegative integer stop sequences, and chronological stop times across the complete source. The limited router supports service times through 47:59 and rejects later times. GTFS times above 24:00 remain service-day offsets; they are never wrapped to midnight. Minute-precision projection rejects nonzero seconds rather than silently rounding. Only Route 00 Orange (`BryOrange`) and stops 126, 127, 134, 142, 151 and 154 are projected. Trips with fewer than two selected stops are omitted. Stop names and coordinates are taken directly from GTFS; they do not establish safe walking connections.

The original `calendar_dates.txt` is empty. Ten explicit 2026 closure dates from https://www.btd.org/fixed-routes/ are overlaid as canceled service: January 1 and 19, February 16, April 3, May 25, September 7, November 26 and 27, December 24 and 25. Provenance distinguishes this website overlay from the original feed. No future-year holidays are inferred. The five-minute transfer margin is an app planning assumption, not a BTD connection guarantee.

The official PDF at https://www.btd.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/BCS_FixedRouteMap.pdf and GTFS contain differing timetable values. The projection preserves the GTFS values and records the unresolved discrepancy in provenance. Do not describe these trips as provider-confirmed. The user-facing planner must flag affected results for confirmation. Preserve original ZIP and source attribution when reviewing changes; compare changed stops, times, service ranges and calendar exceptions before publication.

For reproducibility, run the importer twice and compare output hashes (or check the generated-file diff). Run `npm run data:validate` and the transit tests after integration. A successful import establishes structural consistency only, not actual vehicle operation, food availability, eligibility or pedestrian accessibility.
