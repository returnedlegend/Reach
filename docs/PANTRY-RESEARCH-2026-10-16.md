# Pantry pin research

Checked 2026-10-16. The Brazos Valley Food Bank's [Public Pantry Distributions](https://www.bvfb.org/find-food) page was fetched directly. It identifies all six locations as food distributions, not merely offices, and publishes the service area, hours, distribution format, visit limit, and contact number. Its disclaimer says IDs and proof of income/address may be requested but are never required; pantries may close when food runs out. No organization was contacted, and no entrance or walking route was reviewed.

## Limits and access notes

For all six BVFB-listed pantries, its non-requirement disclaimer applies as above. First Baptist publishes registration 8–10am; A Hopeful Harvest's provider says registration begins at 2:30pm. For other locations no registration requirement or cutoff was found in the reviewed sources. Drive-through listings do not establish walk-up service. BVFB identifies walk-in pickup only for A Hopeful Harvest and Salvation Army; neither source documents step-free entrance/accessibility. First Baptist says volunteers help load dry goods; this does not confirm an accessible pedestrian entrance. First United's first-Saturday schedule has a provider-published game-day exception, and the listed start time conflicts with BVFB's map feature. Hours are food distributions, not office hours, and availability can end early if food runs out.

Coordinates in the accompanying verification record are published/linked feature or destination points. They do not establish an entrance, route, crossing, or walking itinerary. No coordinate was inferred from a Maps camera center, and no place ID was invented.
