# Navigation and model-provider integration research

Transit research was checked 2026-10-23; current DeepSeek model and API documentation was rechecked 2026-10-25. This remains a research note: no transit feed was downloaded or imported, no live inference call was made for this document, and no new transit coverage is enabled. Reach currently routes scheduled transit only on BTD Route 00 Orange; see [GTFS provenance](GTFS.md), [TX-10 data audit](TX10-DATA.md), and the source/fare conflict in [ACCESS-INTEGRATIONS.md](ACCESS-INTEGRATIONS.md).

## What is available and what Reach covers

| Brazos Transit District (BTD) | Its official schedule page and GTFS publication are documented in [GTFS.md](GTFS.md) and [ACCESS-INTEGRATIONS.md](ACCESS-INTEGRATIONS.md). Its fixed routes are open to the general public; ADA paratransit has a separate qualification and service-area test.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Reach projects Route 00 Orange only. | CapMetro (Austin region)      | CapMetro's [Developer Tools page](https://www.capmetro.org/developertools) says it publishes GTFS-formatted schedules plus vehicle-location, service-alert, trip-update, scheduled-service, and map data through the [State of Texas Open Data Portal](https://data.texas.gov/browse?Dataset-Category_Agency=Capital+Metropolitan+Transportation+Authority&category=Transportation&utf8=%E2%9C%93). Its terms describe a revocable, limited right to use and redistribute listed data, “as is/as available”; they reserve the right to alter or remove it. This page identifies data types and the portal, but does not itself specify a stable feed URL, update cadence, or a current feed's validity period. | No CapMetro feed, route geometry, fare, or schedule is integrated. The TX-10 service-area polygon includes the Austin region's longitude range, but Reach has not intersected current CapMetro route/stop geometry against its service-area or pantry polygons. Treat CapMetro as a candidate source for a separate reviewed coverage audit, not as an available route in the planner. |
| TX-10 food providers          | TX-10 records are food sites, not a transit agency/feed. Their access rules and pickup schedules are site-specific, as described in [TX10-DATA.md](TX10-DATA.md).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | A food site's eligibility does not establish eligibility for a transit service or a route connection. Current transit integration remains BTD Route 00 only; do not infer routes from pantry locations, regional boundaries, or provider schedules.                                                                                                                                    |

CapMetro's [service-area map](https://www.capmetro.org/ride/plan/plan/servicearea), [schedules and maps](https://www.capmetro.org/ride/plan/schedmap), and [accessibility information](https://www.capmetro.org/accessibility) are useful for a future human review of geographic and rider-access constraints. CapMetro Access is a distinct ADA paratransit service: CapMetro describes eligibility for people whose disability prevents use of regular bus/rail and references a 0.75-mile fixed-route corridor. Do not model it as an ordinary public fixed route or assume a rider is eligible. A route-feed integration would still need an identified current static feed and real-time endpoints, reviewed coverage and service periods, fare policy, attribution/license review, and routing tests before production use.
