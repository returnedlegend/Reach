# TX-10 regional pickup-site research

Reviewed 2026-10-22 UTC. Four regional sites are confirmed by official nonprofit pages and recorded in `data/research/tx10-regional-candidates.json`. Each published pickup address was geocoded with the U.S. Census Geocoder `Public_AR_Current` benchmark, and each returned address-range point was checked against the official 119th Congress TX-10 geometry in `data/geo/tx10-119.geojson`. These coordinates represent interpolated street locations, not surveyed entrances. No providers were contacted, and pedestrian access was not reviewed.

The CTFB [Find Food Now](https://www.centraltexasfoodbank.org/find-food-now) page embeds the Vivery AccessFood locator and describes it as the way to find current nearby locations. CTFB's [Food Distribution page](https://www.centraltexasfoodbank.org/programs/food-distributions) says mobile pantry distributions are free and do not require registration or appointments; it notes that partner schedules and requirements vary. Houston Food Bank's [Find Help](https://www.houstonfoodbank.org/find-help/) page directs users to its [Find Food Map](https://www.houstonfoodbank.org/find-help/find-food-map/) to find pantry partners. These interactive directories do not expose individual listings in their static page content, so I did not treat the general directory pages as evidence for additional sites or bypass their normal public interfaces. The existing eight BVFB/Brazos listings were not duplicated.

## Unresolved lead

Waller Assistance & Restoration Ministries (WARM) has an official [Food Pantry page](https://warmwaller.org/food-pantry) confirming pickup Tuesday–Thursday, 10 a.m.–3 p.m., TEFAP eligibility/documentation, phone (936) 372-5173 and address 40070 Highway 290 Business, Waller, TX 77484. The Census Geocoder returned no match for the published address, and reviewed official pages did not provide a map destination with coordinates. WARM is therefore documented as a pickup lead but omitted from candidate data until its point can be checked against the district geometry.
